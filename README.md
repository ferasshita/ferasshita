### About Me

I am a software engineer and quantum computing Engineer focused on building scalable backend architectures and exploring the next frontier of computation. Over the past five years, I have worked across tech companies and fast-growing startups, specializing in microservices, API design, and cloud deployments. My main priority is always to bridge the gap between deep, complex research and practical, elegant software engineering that solves real-world problems.
Lately, my work has focused on designing systems that handle complex data streams smoothly and experimenting with quantum machine learning algorithms. I have been lucky enough to channel this experience into some meaningful milestones, from authoring a book aimed at making quantum computing accessible, to co-founding a sustainable technology startup that recently won a major global impact competition. For me, the most rewarding part of software development is taking a rough, ambitious concept and building the reliable infrastructure that brings it to life.
When I am not tweaking backend code or running quantum simulations, I like to direct my energy toward community and environmental projects. You will often find me volunteering technical support for local animal welfare initiatives, exploring the historical and cultural sites around Tripoli, or brainstorming circular economy ideas. I am always open to connecting with fellow engineers, researchers, or anyone who enjoys chatting about the future of tech over a good cup of coffee.
